NEAR, replicated · a replication studyNEAR Hackathon, Investment Thesis track · data to 2026-11-10

About thirty teams measured NEAR. Do their numbers agree?

We recomputed 119 numbers published by 20 of them from raw public data, kept what replicates, and built one thesis on it: NEAR is a growth bet on Intents revenue that today offsets 13% to 18% of new supply, and that revenue can be counted on chain.

101 of 119published numbers are within 5% of our recomputation for the day each was published (89 if all are recomputed on our snapshot day)
13–18%of net issuance is offset by captured Intents revenue, depending on the window
4.24MNEAR held by the three revenue wallets, read from an archival node; the total has never fallen
5.5×growth in captured revenue needed before issuance is offset

Snapshot 2026-11-11. Every figure on this page is generated by python3 -m replicated.page from raw data committed with its hashes. The full argument, with the evidence against, is in THESIS.md.

Re-checked every day. Newest snapshot 2026-11-12 (data to 2026-11-11, 2 snapshots so far): all 5 kill conditions hold; 90-day on-chain cover of net issuance 18.3%; past days changed by DefiLlama since the snapshot before: 0. Every snapshot side by side.

3 · Revenue

The revenue is on chain, and it is all still there

Reported revenue does not have to be taken on trust. It arrives as NEAR in three wallets, and their balances at past block heights can be read from any archival node.

4 · Usage

Usage left the base layer and went to Intents

Two charts, two scales: transactions and dollars are not comparable on one axis. Intents volume and fees come from one upstream source (datasets NEAR publishes on Dune, read through DefiLlama), so agreement between teams on these is not independent confirmation.
